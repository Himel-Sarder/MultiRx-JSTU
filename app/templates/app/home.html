Clinic Management Platform

Reception and consultation, working from the same queue.

MultiRx gives the front desk and the doctor one shared patient record, one live serial queue, and a call bell that connects the two — so nobody has to walk down the corridor to ask who is next.

Sign in to your console

Two roles, one system

Your login ID decides which console opens.

Reception Desk

RE + 4 digits

  • Register new patients with the full Additional Patient Record
  • Look up and update existing patients
  • Run the serial queue and send patients in
  • Hear the doctor's call bell from any reception page
  • Review clinic-wide analysis

Doctor Console

HF + 4 digits

  • See who is in the room and who is waiting
  • Open the full patient record before consulting
  • Write problems, examinations, reports and medicines
  • Generate, view, download and print the prescription
  • Call the next patient with one button

How a visit flows

1

Patient arrives

Reception records demographics and the full clinical record, or pulls up an existing patient.

2

Serial assigned

The patient joins the doctor's queue as serial 1, 2, 3 … in arrival order.

3

Consultation

The doctor opens the record, prescribes, and prints or downloads the PDF.

4

Next patient

One button rings reception for 3 seconds; the desk sends the next serial in.
